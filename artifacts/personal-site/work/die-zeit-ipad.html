DIE ZEIT iPad App

A daily reading habit for one of Germany's most respected weekly newspapers.

The DIE ZEIT iPad app extends the weekly newspaper into a daily reading habit. Subscribers get the full issue plus a curated stream of articles between issues, with offline reading and a markedly faster article-open time than the previous app.

Highlights

  • Section navigation that mirrors the printed paper, so readers don't need to learn a new mental model.
  • Reading position synced across devices.
  • Subscription paywall designed to be felt only at the right moments.

What I learned

Newspaper readers value predictability over novelty. The product changes that worked best were the ones that made the existing ritual smoother, not the ones that invented new rituals.
