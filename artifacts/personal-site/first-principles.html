First principles reasoning

Elon Musk answers how he succeeds in pursuing such groundbreaking ideas and how he has been able to innovate so often.

A Lesson in Life ›
21.03.2013

Elon Musk attributes much of his ability to innovate to first-principles thinking — boiling problems down to fundamental truths and reasoning up from there, rather than reasoning by analogy.

"I think it is important to reason from first principles rather than by analogy. … You boil things down to the most fundamental truths and then reason up from there."

The tradeoff is speed: analogy is fast, first principles is slow but produces non-obvious answers. Most of the time analogy is fine. But once or twice a year a problem is worth taking the slow path on.

A small practice

Whenever I catch myself saying "that's how it's always done" I write the sentence on a card and force myself to defend it from scratch. Half the time the defense doesn't hold up.
