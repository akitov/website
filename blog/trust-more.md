title: You lose less by trusting more
excerpt: Managing a business is not about asserting control to minimize costs and risks, but about working collaboratively to achieve an agreed goal.
---

Early in my career I thought of management as a defensive discipline: minimize cost, minimize risk, control variance. The harder I clamped down, the slower we moved and the less my team brought to the table.

> Trust is a leading indicator of velocity.

When I let go of approval gates and gave teammates the chance to ship without my pre-approval, two things happened:

1. We moved noticeably faster.
2. The number of "expensive" mistakes went *down*, not up — because people felt accountable.

The losses from misplaced trust have always been smaller than the losses from over-control.
