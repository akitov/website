ZEIT MAGAZIN is the weekend supplement of one of Germany's most respected weekly newspapers. Translating its long-form, photo-driven storytelling onto the iPad meant rebuilding the reading experience from the ground up — not just porting the print layout.

## What we changed

- A single vertical reading column with generous typography, instead of a faux-page grid.
- Photo essays presented as full-bleed swipeable galleries.
- Background syncing of issues so the magazine is ready before the reader opens the app.

## What we kept

The voice of the magazine. Every typographic and editorial choice was made with the print art director in the room.
