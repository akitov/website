An Event in Life ›
07.01.2013

Speaking at TEDxBG in 2012 was the first time I tried to articulate the why behind betahaus to an audience that didn't already know the space. Twelve minutes is short — long enough to tell one story, not nearly long enough to defend it.

The talk centered on a single observation: coworking is not really about desks. It is about taking the loneliness out of building something. The desks are a side effect.

Slides I still keep

  • A photo of an empty hall, three weeks before opening.
  • The same hall, three weeks after, with thirty people I had never met working side by side.
  • A receipt for the first cup of coffee we sold.

That sequence still does the job better than any deck I could write today.
