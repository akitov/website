betahaus | Sofia

Creating and establishing the first coworking space in Sofia.

betahaus is the first coworking space in Sofia, Bulgaria. We are creating a working environment for freelancers, entrepreneurs and startups and the physical epicenter for most of the city's entrepreneurial activities. Of course it is all about added value and product experience. There is no way otherwise for such a product in the real estate business to open up a completely new market and take off in six months, break even, hold hundreds of events and host such an array of companies.

Workspace

Everybody shares a nice, open working space with the right atmosphere for their creative minds. The room layout, the music, the temperature of the espresso machine — every detail is part of a single product.

Before and after

A photographic project documented the transformation of the building from an empty industrial loft into the heart of Sofia's startup scene. The "before" photos make me appreciate the "after" every time I walk in.
